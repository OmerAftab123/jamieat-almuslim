# Scoring methodology

## Criteria (0–4 each)

| Key | Criterion | What a high score means |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `islamic` | Islamic alignment | Typical pathways fit common Islamic values (general, not a ruling). |
| `benefit` | Benefit to people and society | Field contributes to welfare and problem solving. |
| `financial` | Financial lucrativeness | Realistic earnings and progression are strong. |
| `futureResilience` | Future resilience | Likely to stay relevant as technology, industries and society change. |
| `aiResilience` | AI resilience | Tasks are less exposed to AI-driven automation (higher = more resilient). |
| `employmentStability` | Employment stability | Stable, sustainable work across economic cycles. |
| `flexibility` | Flexibility | Transferable skills and many alternative careers or locations. |

Future resilience and AI resilience are scored separately on purpose. One asks about long-term relevance, the other about automation exposure.

## Score meanings

| Score | Label |
| --- | --- |
| 0 | MUST NOT |
| 1 | SHOULD NOT |
| 2 | CAN / MAY |
| 3 | SHOULD |
| 4 | MUST |

These labels are a **scale**. They describe the strength of the case on that criterion. They are not religious rulings. No score makes a degree obligatory or forbidden.

## Overall score

- Default weights: equal (1/7 each).
- Custom weights are normalised so they sum to 100%. All weights at zero is rejected.
- Overall = Σ (score × normalised weight), on the 0–4 scale.
- If any criterion is missing, overall is **not calculated**. Missing is never 0.
- Display: one decimal place. Ordering: the unrounded value.

## Ranking

- Descending by the unrounded overall score.
- Competition ranking: tied scores share the best rank and the next rank is skipped (`1, =2, =2, 4`).
- Families with missing data are listed last with no rank (shown as —).
- Single-criterion ranking uses the same tie rules and excludes missing values.

## Confidence

`low`, `medium` or `high`, per family. A family's confidence reflects how well its scores are supported by checked evidence. All current families are `analyst-draft`.
