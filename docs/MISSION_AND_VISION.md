# Mission and vision

## Mission

Help Muslim students, especially those in Pakistan considering international undergraduate study, make careful decisions about degrees, careers, universities and funding, using evidence they can inspect.

## Vision

A free, open, reproducible research tool in which every figure is sourced and dated, missing information is shown as missing, and no student is told a degree is "required" or a scholarship is "guaranteed" when the evidence does not say so.

## Principles

1. **Verified or labelled.** A value is either verified with a source and date, or it is labelled as an estimate, analyst draft, or not verified.
2. **Missing is not zero.** A missing score or cost never counts as 0.
3. **Judgement stays visible.** Career scores are analyst judgements with rationale, assumptions, confidence and change factors.
4. **Religious alignment is one criterion.** It describes fit with common Islamic values in a general way. It never makes a degree obligatory or forbidden, and this platform does not issue rulings.
5. **Privacy by design.** The matcher runs in the browser. There are no accounts and no tracking.
6. **Free to run.** No paid AI APIs and no database are needed for the current version.

## Non-goals

- Admissions decisions, guaranteed admission, or competitiveness predictions.
- Visa or legal advice.
- Replacing official university, funder or government sources.
