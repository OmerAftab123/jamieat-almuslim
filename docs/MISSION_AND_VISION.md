# Mission and vision

## Mission

Help Muslim students make informed decisions about degrees, careers and universities by making the evidence visible: what a degree is documented to lead to, what a university officially publishes, what funding is documented, what it costs, and what is still unknown.

The first audience is students from Pakistan who are considering **international undergraduate** study. The default country in the matcher is Pakistan and the default pathway is undergraduate.

## Vision

A free, open, reproducible research resource that:

- shows its sources, dates and verification status for every figure;
- treats missing information as missing, never as zero or as a default;
- gives students a structured way to weigh religious alignment alongside career, financial, social and resilience factors;
- can be maintained by volunteers through reviewable data files and public issues.

## Principles

1. **Verified data only.** A figure appears only if it has a recorded source and a date. Otherwise it is labelled "not verified" or "not researched".
2. **No invented records.** No university, programme, tuition figure, scholarship, acceptance rate, salary or religious ruling is made up to fill a gap.
3. **Scores are judgements.** The 0–4 scores are documented editorial judgements with a rationale, a confidence level and assumptions. They are not measurements, and they are not verdicts.
4. **Religious alignment is one dimension.** It is scored separately and never presented as a religious requirement. A degree is never described as "mandatory" or "forbidden" because of its score.
5. **Future resilience is separate from AI resilience.** They are scored as two criteria.
6. **Student data stays private.** The matcher runs in the browser. The site has no accounts, no tracking and no server-side storage of profiles.
7. **Free and simple.** Static website, open-source tools, no paid AI APIs, no paid database.
8. **Honest about limits.** The site does not guarantee admission, funding, employment or salary, and is not an admissions, visa or legal service.

## What success looks like

- Every university record shows its official QS rank and date, with ties kept as published.
- Each programme, admissions requirement and funding route that is shown has a source link and a last-checked date.
- Students can see at a glance which parts of their question are answered and which are not.
- Corrections from the community can be made through a reviewable pull request.

## Non-goals (for now)

- Personalised admissions chances or competitiveness predictions.
- Visa, legal, immigration or religious advice.
- Paid advertising or lead generation.
- Collecting personal data on a server.
